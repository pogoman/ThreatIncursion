What the intel panel can actually draw
Learned from the round-one build running in-game. Round two is designed against this kit and nothing else.

Rules round two follows
Budget
Design width 1,300 px at 1080p; the narrow variant shows the fold at 1,100 px. Every column has a fixed pixel width and a hard character cap; anything longer goes to the tooltip, never to "...".
Ledger rows are custom panels, 30 px tall: rank, name, one chip, size boxes, a bar, pips, three numbers, up to three crests. No sentence anywhere on a row.
Sector strip is one line of eight label/value pairs. Phase bar is inline in the header, 260 px.
Icons over words
Inbound = faction crests (who is coming) with a "+n" overflow; outbound = a red count; organs = the real industry icons, red when disrupted, day count beneath; worlds = size digits in boxes, not "4 (s4 4 4 3)".
Status words are a fixed vocabulary: mustering - recall, en route, bombarding, engaging, withdrawing.
Depth on hover
Row tooltip: system map, every operation as a sentence, systems in reach, distance to your colonies. Card tooltip: the decline forecast, the Core-recovers verdict, supplies and draws, fuel costs explained. Header tooltips define each column.
Selected system
Colony cards 2-across, 112 px each, six worlds fit in three rows; operations as a 4-column table capped at nine rows with "+n more"; one button row. At narrow widths the operations table drops below the cards.
Placement: a permanent entry in Major events named The Threat War Effort, present while any hive or seeding swarm exists.
