## Decisions (the user's)

- **How a run is scored** (2026-10-02) Humans: forward bases set up and held, at its simplest the number held at the end of the run. Threat: colonies founded plus size levels gained during the run, weighted up the scale (7 to 8 counts more than 2 to 3), so a run from an established save scores too. Both: worlds destroyed (hives by humans; human colonies and forward bases by the Threat), so neither side scores by sitting passive. The weights are to be played with; start on spread alone and watch what emerges. Offline simulator approved the same day, with the shared rules package, two more planner runs and monthly dumps. -> `war-sim.md` 7, memory `run-scoring`
- **What makes a run good? (user, 2026-10-02)** Dynamic and emergent, not deterministic: variation between runs is fine as long as the average is good. Judge a run by its whole outcome, never by its deviation from an expected run. Best: both sides winning and losing ground back and forth. Good: one side annihilating the other. Bad: neither side attacking. Worst: a stalemate, two sides failing to change the situation for years. So a balance change is scored over many seeds on how much the map changes hands and how long the longest dead stretch is, beside the two sides' scores. -> `war-sim.md` 7
- **Lead measure: destruction, not ownership switches (user, 2026-10-02).** Judge liveliness by successful colony destruction on both sides - hives killed by the humans, worlds and forward bases destroyed by the swarm, per year - and by `mutual`, the smaller of the two rates (zero when either side sits passive). Reversals and contested systems are secondary. The real runs already read this way: planner wars destroy on both sides (pd9a 24 hives against 8 worlds + 14 bases, pd10a 45 against 19 + 58), council wars only on the swarm's (0-2 hives). -> `war-sim.md` 7

## Siege sizing and targeting (NPC)

- **How big must an NPC siege be, and why are there 10-20k FP fleets?** Defense Swarm FP faced x `npcSiegeOrbitMargin` (1.5), no fleet cap; fleets are at most difficulty 10 (250 FP). FP faced = max(strongest target world, the SUM of every garrison in the system while `npcSiegeOrbitSystem` is on, swarms met by its last called-off siege within `siegeMetMemoryDays` 90). Player sieges need 0. `IncursionManager.siegeOrbitNeeded` / `siegeOrbitWeighed` / `siegeFleetSizes` -> `ground-war-runs.md` "Overnight 2026-09-29"
- **Are there really 20k FP fleets?** No fleet that size sails: 18-26k is what an NPC believes a core siege needs. Fleets grow in whole fleets of 6-10 points x 25 FP (mean 1.84x the need), and the median NPC fuel pool is 28k against 0.4 fuel per FP per ly. h48a: all 7 launches hit 1-2-hive systems needing under 4.3k. `IncursionManager.siegeFleetGoal`
- **Why are big multi-hive core systems never sieged?** The whole-system sum x 1.5 dwarfs any one world (h48a: cores 12-17k FP). The siege waits and posts a swarm bounty instead. Since 2026-10-01 sieges size per world at 1.5x from the faction's report (`siegeOrbitWeighed`), and raids bomb the worlds a siege does not take. Under the planner a system no siege of the faction can take still posts the bounty, through the launch's gates or `IncursionManager.orbitBounty` once the plan's chance is met. `IncursionManager.systemSwarms`, `ThreatSwarmBountyIntel.post` -> `attack-planner.md` "Why: what h48a showed"
- **Do a system's garrisons converge on a besieged world?** No, so the whole-system sizing rests on a false premise. A world's reserve never leaves it: what moves is FP above 1.25x a colony's want and one swarm, plus, for a system under attack, one fleet at a time from a quiet colony at its want; in a core the want is the reserve (Pelephanar: want 11,032, need 200-466; Bastion/Command x2/x3 the reserve). A core world's own orbit holds 5-21% of its system, 0.9-2.5k. `ThreatPosture.wantFP`, `ThreatColonyManager.redistributeByPressure` -> `attack-planner.md` "Why: what h48a showed"
- **Where are the openings?** Surplus moves as real fleets, the same system's colonies first, then the nearest, landing 0-15 days after the 5-day pass that sends it. A quiet colony gives a system under attack one fleet at a time, never below its reserve, and refills from its bank between (1-5 days while the bank pays and its Core and Nexus stand); a fresh seed holds one swarm of 63-67 FP. No faction strikes these. -> `attack-planner.md` "Why: what h48a showed"
- **What does it take to raid (bomb) a hive?** The larger of two: the guns need F >= D/29 for a day to buy a day down (42 FP at size 2, 435 at size 7, 498 at size 8), and the orbit must be uncontested, so more than 2x the swarms over that world (130 for a fresh seed, 2-5k for a core world). So 300 FP reaches fresh seeds, stripped and soft hives, never a core. A 300 FP raid 12 ly out and back costs about 2,900 fuel and 360 supplies. -> `attack-planner.md` "4. Raids"
- **How does an off-screen siege resolve?** A siege expedition (`ThreatPurgeFGI`; an NPC's, and the player's, which never calls off) runs a day at a time since 2026-10-01 (knob `abstractSiegeDaily`): `advanceDaily` takes it on arrival; each day over each world it weighs `ThreatGroundFronts.hostileFleetsNear`, goes home outweighed (`callOff`), fights a day (`ThreatAbstractBattle.foughtDay`, both sides lose; since 2026-10-02 its play's hunts at the world fight beside it and share the loss, and it waits up to 3 days for hunts still coming, `ThreatSoftening.playFleetsNear`), bombards a day (`abstractSiegeStep`) while the orbit is its own, then lands through vanilla's passes (`endWorld`); every toll is route damage. The swarm's strike still resolves in one frame (`resolveOnArrival` -> `ThreatGroundFronts.abstractSiege`) -> `ground-war-code-paths.md` section 8
- **What does the Threat read about humans remotely?** Since the swarm's fog (`threatinc_swarmFogOfWar`, 2026-10-01), only what its eyes (a hive, Threat fleet, strike route or Threat front in the system), radar (Bastion and Command worlds, `swarmRadarRangeLY`) and Scouting Swarms saw: `ThreatSwarmIntel` contacts (attacks) and places (staging, guards, reach, defence), aged by `intelHalfLifeDays`. Every remote read, by symbol, and what stayed live: `threat-fog.md` section 1. With the fog off, as before: exactly and live: each staging base's fuel and supplies (as siege FP against the hive it stages for, so the swarm reinforces before anything sails), the FP of every NPC attack and order from dispatch, at any distance (a siege from dispatch too since 2026-10-01, the user's decision 10: while its route is abstract it counts by its flotilla, `abstractNow`, until its fleets spawn, `ThreatPosture.countsAbstract`, knob `threatSeesBookedSieges`; before, an off-screen daily siege added no attack FP: Gamma Sonora under a 4,900 FP daily siege read "attacks 0"), forward guards, convoy FP and routes (`ThreatRaiders`), a target's defenders and station. `ThreatPosture.siegeCapacityFP` -> `attack-planner.md` "9. Decisions for the user"
- **What is a siege's fuel bill made of?** About 85% passage (FP/25 x ly x 10) and 15% tactical ordnance (0.04 x FP x days, at most 120), billed for the worst single target, not summed over the system. Razing prices (100k-1M) are separate and chosen only where cheaper than landing. Marines are sized after bombardment (`raidStrNeededAt`). `IncursionManager.expeditionFuel` / `siegeOrdnance`
- **How are NPC siege fleets paid at spawn?** The game's real built FP is compared with the estimate: extra drawn from home then donors, overpay refunded, warships trimmed if the reserve cannot pay (flagship and transports never cut); supplies come back per fleet on arrival home. Fuel and ordnance stay at the estimate. `ThreatPurgeFGI.setLedger` / `settleLedger`
- **What does "Raze or siege of X from Y" in the log mean?** Only the weighing, printed for every base considered; not a launch.
- **When do NPCs pick siege targets?** Since 2026-10-01 the attack planner picks them (`attackPlanner`; the monthly `tryPurgeBombardments` pass returns at once). Each faction plans every `planIntervalDays` 7 days, and on news: a prong resolved, a raid over, a report that moved by half, `huntThinned`. Pressed system first, then the landing, then the reported orbit; bases cheapest first; one new siege per system per plan. Sieges are added until P(at least one lands) >= `planConfidence` 0.8, where each prong's P is its report's trust at arrival. `purgeCooldownDays` 240 per colony and the `siegeAllowed` stance gate still apply. The Threat never reads NPC targets. `ThreatAttackPlanner.plan` -> `attack-planner.md` sections 2 and 11. Before the planner: the slow tick (`tickDays` 30), easiest first, one launch per system per pass, no plan.
- **Which worlds does one siege take?** `siegeTargets`: the whole system if the base can take and pay its orbit; else the most of the easiest it can afford; else the first it can take alone; else the easiest alone (convoys stage toward it). Excludes worlds on cooldown, booked (`bookedWorlds`), held by another faction's army, or whose own front finishes first. `IncursionManager.siegeTargets` -> `strategy-reserves-sieges.md` "Reserves"
- **Do humans attack one target at a time?** No. Since 2026-10-01 a faction's plan spreads sieges over several systems until P(at least one lands) >= 0.8, and raids bomb the worlds no siege books. One siege takes one system's worlds in turn with its whole flotilla; a faction can run several sieges per system on unbooked worlds, and in many systems at once. Limits: one new siege per system per plan, one hunt per faction per system, one Support or Defend per world, and one raid per faction per world, kept apart from Support. `IncursionManager.bookedWorlds`, `ThreatSoftening.hunting`, `ThreatFleetOrders.hasSupport` / `hasRaid` -> `attack-planner.md` section 11, `strategy-reserves-sieges.md` "Reserves"
- **Is a siege's trip reserved at launch?** No. `siegeCanPay` prices deposit + supplies for `siegeTripDays`, then `ThreatUpkeep` pays as it goes, so two sieges admitted in one pass can count the same stock; an over-committed one starves home (`outOfSupplies`). Unspawned expeditions are billed monthly (`chargeAbstract`). -> `strategy-hunting-reach.md` "The factions' reach and stance"
- **Why does an NPC siege turn home without landing?** No front down and hostile swarms >= `siegeBreakOffRatio` (1.0) x its live FP over any target (unspawned: the same test in vanilla's `WarSimScript` units). It calls off, remembers the swarms for 90 days and posts a bounty. It never diverts (targets fixed at launch); player sieges never break off. `ThreatPurgeFGI.breaksOff` / `breaksOffAbstract` / `callOff` -> `ground-war-sieges.md` "Bombardment v2"
- **Can a siege expedition be redirected?** No. `ThreatPurgeFGI` targets (`where`/`allowedTargets`) are fixed at launch; it is one route, abstract until the player is within 1.6 ly, and `spawnFleets` deletes the route for good. It ends by return, `callOff`, `outOfSupplies`, `abortPurgesAgainst` or its last fleet detaching. Per-fleet moves exist (`recallFleet`, `detachToHunt` -> `ThreatFleetOrders.adoptHunt`). Retargeting an abstract siege means route and ledger surgery with no vanilla precedent. -> `attack-planner.md` "3. On arrival"
- **Is there an NPC bomb-only fleet that softens targets?** Yes since 2026-10-01: the RAID, a Support order flagged `raid` (`ThreatFleetOrders.dispatchRaid`, sent by `ThreatAttackPlanner`), bombs a hive with no front of its own and leaves on the contest, the commander's stop or a third lost (`raidOver`). Before it, no (the user remembered one). NPC orders are guard, support, defend, hunt; bombing happens only as a siege's squadron, tactical slices while a siege waits to land, or a Support fleet (`dispatchOrbit`, triggered by `ThreatConvoys.supportFor` only with an owned front and a refused orbit) whose `tickSupport` -> `supportSlice` bombs while the orbit is clear; `supportLost` stands it down unless `navyHoldsOver`. Hunts fight swarms and never bomb. -> `attack-planner.md` "4. Raids" and section 11
- **Why have NPCs never saturated a hive?** h48a: the squadron cut planned fuel a median 9x, yet 0 saturations and the runtime path never ran - pools cannot pay even the flotilla's passage, and the verdict is all-or-nothing (priced with `Float.MAX_VALUE` fuel, then gated on spare). `ThreatGroundFronts.squadronPlan`
- **What can redirect a fleet?** NPC: only `ThreatSoftening.divert` (a hunt outmatched at muster or moving on; no `sectorKnows` check), `ThreatFleetOrders.divertRaid` (a raid turned back in its first two days at the world, to its fallback, 2026-10-01) and `ThreatResponseIntel.retarget` (target hive dead -> nearest found hive). Sieges can only abort; Threat strikes are never redirected. Only the player's Support/Defend/Hunt reassign fleets (`nearestReassignable`, `takeOver`, `adoptHunt`). -> `strategy-hunting-reach.md` "Hunting forces"
- **How far does a siege fleet roam?** Hunts only within `siegeHuntRange` 1500 of a contested world, recalled beyond `siegeLeashRange` 6000 or out of the system; `ALLOW_LONG_PURSUIT` is stripped. `ThreatFleetOrders.siegeLeash` -> `ground-war-fronts.md` "NPC fronts"
- **When does a faction mobilise and stand down?** NPC: when a Threat strike with one of its worlds in the sweep is detected (`IncursionManager.onStrikeDetected` -> `ThreatWarState.recordStrike`; every faction in the sweep since 2026-10-02, before that the first target's alone); and every one, struck or not, once the swarm reaches `mobiliseAtPhase` 3 (`IncursionManager.mobiliseAtPhase`, 2026-10-02); `warExcludedFactions` (pirates) never. Player: by button. Stands down only if `warModeStandDownDays` > 0, the last strike is that old, and no found hive is in reach (`hiveInReach`). -> `strategy-reserves-sieges.md` "War mode"
- **How does a faction's stance work?** `ThreatFactionStance.evaluate` every 7 days. CONSOLIDATE: pressed (struck within 60 d or a Threat front on its world) and losing or outweighed; no new links, sieges only at systems facing it. PRESS: not pressed, force >= `stancePressRatio` 1.5 x the swarm facing it, a siege payable; its system weighed first. Else EXPAND. Held `stanceDwellDays` 30 except into CONSOLIDATE. Priority only, never a cap. Until 2026-10-01 a `> 0` guard on the negative clock timestamp read every strike as "never", so pressed meant a Threat front only (123 of 123 logged pressed lines); fixed to `!= 0`. `lastStruckTimestamp` is the mobilisation time until a first strike. -> `strategy-hunting-reach.md` "The factions' reach and stance"

- **Where is the NPC war code's call graph?** The siege pass and its gates, targets, orbit sizing, the launch, the stance, orders and the Support stand-down, hunts, the coalition call and what is saved, by symbol. -> `strategy-code-paths.md`
- **Which gates does calling `launchSiegeExpedition` directly skip?** The pass's own: `sectorKnows`, `reliefOwed`, `siegeAllowed`, `anySiegeReady`, the `siegeTargets` filters (booked, held by another army, cooldown), `siegeDifficulty` / `siegeSizesFor`, and the launch notice and log. Inside the launch stay the orbit gate and the provisions gate (both post the bounty), marines and armaments; a postponement stamps no cooldown. -> `strategy-code-paths.md` "Injecting a planned siege"
- **Who must agree on `siegeOrbitNeeded`?** Five readers: the launch's orbit gate, `siegeFleetGoal`, `siegeAffordable`, `ThreatFleetOrders.dispatchOrbit`, `ThreatConvoys.supportFor`. -> `strategy-code-paths.md` "Orbit sizing"
- **Why does an NPC Support over a hive without its own front go home at once?** `ThreatFleetOrders.poll`'s `supportLost`: `ThreatGroundFronts.navyHoldsOver` is false wherever the faction owns no front, so it stands down within half a day. A raid order must be exempt. -> `strategy-code-paths.md` "Orders"
- **Is the siege cooldown per world?** Yes (`onPurgeCooldown` over `lastPurgeTimes`; the launch stamps every target), but the pass weighs each system once and launches at most one siege per system per pass. -> `strategy-code-paths.md` "Cooldown"

## War council (NPC strategy)

- **What decides an NPC's attacks while `warCouncil` is on?** `ThreatWarCouncil` picks a strategy and a focus cluster each review; `ThreatPlays.plan` starts plays from them. The attack planner (`ThreatAttackPlanner.active`) and the monthly siege pass (`tryPurgeBombardments`) stand aside. `war-council.md` s16.
- **How does a play skip the enemy-FP gates?** Through its `playId`. `launchSiegeExpedition`'s 10-argument overload drops the orbit gate, the orbit term and the Coalition Call; `ThreatSoftening.sendPlay` builds a held `Force` (`hold`) paid from `playPayableFP`; `ThreatPurgeFGI.friendsNear` counts the play's hunts against a break-off. With `playId` null every path is unchanged. `war-council.md` s16.
- **Who sets the stance of a governed faction?** The council (`ThreatFactionStance.set`; `refresh` skips governed factions): Hold is CONSOLIDATE, Starve, Roll back and Decapitate are PRESS, EXPAND with no play running.
- **How does a play stage?** `ThreatConvoys.stageForPlay` overrides `stagingHive` and `siegeStock`'s wants for that base until `clearPlayStaging` (the siege sails or the play ends). A feint's strike base stages against the decoy.
- **How does a play learn which raids were driven off?** `ThreatPlays.raidEnded`, called from `ThreatFleetOrders.endRaid`, `standDown` and the destroyed path, books each `raidId` once.
- **What ends a held play force?** `ThreatSoftening.release` (its play lets it go), or a stand-down: the play ending (`end` stands down forces that never went in), its play missing, or `warCouncil` off (`advanceForce`).
- **Which base sends a play's squadron?** `ThreatPlays.squadronBase`: the nearest base of the faction that reaches the world and pays the squadron whole (`sortieReachFP`). A raid pays from one base with no donors, and the nearest base (`pickBase`) is often an empty forward base. In h53b a bombing campaign with 35k fuel a month sent nothing for two months.
- **How long does a hammer wait at the muster?** The voyage (`ThreatReach.days` from its base to the target) plus `councilMusterDays` x `musterMult` x jitter. In h53b a 30-day muster 39 ly out filled 0 of 750 FP.
- **What did the first council runs show (h53a-b, 2026-10-01)?** While few hives were known, both factions read ahead (Hegemony 2.0, Persean 1.7) and drew Decapitate. Hegemony#3's hammer ran its whole cycle: 2.5k FP of hunts, a 5.6k FP siege, the Nexus down 55 world-days, orbit held 38 days, no landing; success. Once the swarm reached 16 hives (weight ~95 against single-faction weights of 66-71), both read outmatched (0.66-0.76) and held for the rest of the run, the Hegemony on 338k fuel. One faction's weight against the whole known swarm puts every faction in Hold once the swarm passes about 16 hives.
- **Where does a bombing campaign's squadron go?** Only to a Nexus that `ThreatPlays.bombable` allows: the faction's reports show no swarm over it, or its flotilla holds the orbit. The least guarded goes first, and the saturation siege sails from `richestBase` (the cluster's base with the largest `siegeCapacityFP`). In h53c, squadrons sent alone into 600-3500 FP of swarms were all driven off, and the core's saturation share from the nearest outpost was about 500 FP, too small to sail.
- **Why did the human council do so little in pd2a (1 hive killed in 8.6 years)?** Hold won 70% of months: pressed (true 85% of the time) and outmatched each added +2 to Hold and pressed halved Starve, defeating "Starve about as high as Hold"; Hold never starts a hammer. Bombers of opportunity failed 48 of 55 on one-day "unguarded" snapshots of colonies still being garrisoned, and never read what they learned. Both fixed 2026-10-01 (`ThreatWarCouncil.scores` counts pressure once; `ThreatPlays.opportunity` weighs `learned` and rests a world that drove a squadron off, `REPULSED_FLAG`). Still open for the user: Starve's orbit is circular, hammer sieges are small beside their hunts. pd4a (with the fixes): HOLD 58%, STARVE 38%, no repeated bombers, but still 1 hive killed; then human supplies ran dry (17k a month to 0) and a siege fought alone beside its own hunts. pd6a (hunts fight beside the siege): unexercised, the hunts were let go after the siege had landed (fixed: `ThreatPlays.siegeNear`, live ETA) or stood down out of supplies. pd8a: hunts beside the siege on 27 of 42 fight days, but 4 sieges, 0 landed. -> `war-council-runs.md` 1-3
- **Does the swarm run away in a new game?** In pd2a (fog on) it went from 5 to 139 hives in about 103 months (doubling every ~18 months to month 90, then +3 a month), took 16 human worlds and kept 95k FP banked and 250k FP of fleets; its stance was never CONSOLIDATE. The fog-off twin pd3a grew bigger (205 hives at month 103) with the same strike rate per hive, so the fog is not the cause; the fogged swarm only stops garrisoning ahead of attacks it has not seen. -> `threat-fog.md` 4, `war-council-runs.md` 1
- **Why is the swarm 5x smaller with the council off (pd7a, 2026-10-02)?** With `warCouncil` off the attack planner sails about 2 sieges a month from month 39 (202 by month 97, 43 landed, 27 hives killed), which keeps `ThreatStance` in CONSOLIDATE (63 of 119 months) and so stops founding: 30 hives at month 97 against 155-201 in the council runs (1-2 hives killed, 15-27 sieges). The council replaces the planner by design (`ThreatAttackPlanner.active` false) and only HAMMER plays land, one major play per faction at a time (`ThreatPlays.major`), none under Hold. Supplies are not the cause. Open for the user. -> `war-council-runs.md` 4
- **Why won't the swarm strike a world it has never seen?** Under the swarm's fog a strike candidate needs a `ThreatSwarmIntel` place (`IncursionManager.strikeSeen`); `targetDefence` is the place's `defenceFP`, `Float.MAX_VALUE` without one. Places come from eyes, Bastion radar, Scouting Swarms (and re-scouts of stale systems), the once-per-save seeding of old saves' charted systems, or, with scouting off, `seedUnscouted`. -> `threat-fog.md` section 4
- **What does a sighted human base tell the swarm?** Its own stock only: `stagedFP` (`ThreatPosture.stagedBy`, no donors), `reachLY` (`IncursionManager.seenSiegeBaseReachLY`), guards and defence, dated and aged by trust. Remote donor depots were leaking into those figures until the review fix; whether the base counts as staging at all still uses the donor-pooled `siegeBasesFor` (an open yes/no leak). -> `threat-fog.md` section 4
- **Why did the swarm stop claiming systems for a year (pd2a months 37-49)?** First the PRESS stance halved `claimCap` under 6-7 held claims, then fuel: the 3.7-5k a far founding needs went on sector reinforcements (22-27 swarms a month) while blockades cut the fuel made. Re-scouts were suspected (478 Scouting Swarms with the fog on, 17 off) and since 2026-10-02 wait above the dearest pending founding (`ThreatSwarmScouts.claimReserve`), but pd5a stalled 11 months anyway: a generic fuel crunch (pd3a, fog off, hit it too). -> `threat-fog.md` 4
- **Why are bombers of opportunity driven off over worlds reported unguarded?** Until the swarm's fog (2026-10-01, `threat-fog.md`) the Threat saw every human fleet from the day it sailed, so it moved 1.4-2.8k FP of Defense Swarms over the world before the squadron arrived (h53c, every BOMBERS play). Now it sees a force only through its eyes, radar or scouts.
- **Why does Starve do no damage (h53d, 2026-10-01)?** Outmatched factions now draw it, but its saturation siege, sized from the means share (6.7k, 2.4k, 1.7k FP), meets 1.6-2.4x its strength on arrival: the Threat sees it sail and its posture counts it as an attack (Hybrasil wanted 16k, held 19k). The abstract break-off weighs every Threat fleet in the system, so it turns home whole ("Siege Called Off", ordnance unburned) or is caught by a passing strike (Persean, 11% back). No orbit is ever held and every Nexus keeps its garrison, so `bombable` never lets a squadron go, and each campaign ends "ran its course" with 0 Nexus-down days: a failure that learning then holds against Starve. Both h53d hammers failed the same way: Hegemony#11 sent 4.7k FP against 320 FP reported and met 5.4k.
- **What happens when a council's play cannot start on its focus?** `plan` clears the focus, so the next day draws another cluster, and logs once per strategy and play type ("Council f: no TYPE on X can start").
- **Where are the council's knobs?** `threatinc_council*` in settings.json; personalities are `threatinc_councilPersonalities`, keyed by faction id, with `default` as the fallback. Luna exposes `warCouncil`, `councilReviewDays`, `councilTemperature`, `councilLearning`, `councilHammerShare` and `councilSquadronFP`.

## What NPCs know (intel)

- **Is there fog of war or radar on Threat strength?** Yes since 2026-10-01 (`intelFogOfWar`): `ThreatIntel` keeps each mobilised faction's and the player's report of each found system - swarm FP per hive world within 1,500 su, the day and how seen (eyes, radar, scout) - and every human-side reader asks `ThreatIntel.report` / `worldFP` / `trust`, never the garrisons. `ThreatIntel.see` is the one remote read; on-site judgement (contest, break-off, leash) stays live. Off, readers get the live picture. -> `attack-planner.md` sections 1 and 11
- **Which human-side code reads the swarm, and where are the choke points?** Since 2026-10-01 they all read the observer's report (`ThreatIntel.report` / `worldFP` / `worldFleets` / `trust`), and `ThreatIntel.see` is the one remote read. The `ownedFleetFP` leak in `garrisonNowFP` is gone. Still live: the board's Reach "grounded" (hive supply state), its Activity column (raiders, seeding waves) and the convoy rows' "HUNTED" (`attack-planner.md` section 11, "Still read live"). Before the fog, the choke points were `IncursionManager.siegeOrbitFP` (carrying the siege chain, Support, the stance target and hunts), `ThreatSoftening.garrisonNowFP`, the `countLiveGarrison` head-counts (cooldown, difficulty, convoy door, board, contracts, `InfestedSystemIntel`, `ThreatFrontlines.strikeOf`) and `ThreatFactionStance.evaluate`'s "theirs". Never swap `countLiveGarrison` or `garrisonsFor`: the Threat uses them. -> `attack-planner.md` "8a. Every human-side read of the swarm"
- **What does `sectorKnows` gate?** Whether a hive system is found (`ThreatScouts.sectorKnows` over `ThreatIncData.discoveredSystems()`, knob `hiveFogOfWar` on). Gates sieges, the response task force and its retarget, staging, stand-down, contracts, link founding, `hasSiegeableHive`, `siegeBasesFor`, the stance's `weakestTarget`. Does NOT gate `ThreatSoftening` (send, divert), `ThreatCoalition`, `ThreatAid`, or `ThreatPosture`'s attacks, hostiles and losses (its staged term goes through `ThreatConvoys.stagingHive` and its forward term through `ThreatFrontlines.hiveNear`, both gated). The swarm's `ThreatStance.weakTargets` is gated by the swarm's own charts (`ThreatSwarmScouts.swarmKnows`), not this; `weakestTarget` is the human `ThreatFactionStance`'s. The human stance's "theirs" is gated since the planner build (`ThreatIntel.systemFP` over found systems). "Found" is sector-wide, not per faction, and `getAllLiveColonyMarkets` / `totalColonySize` / `countColonies` count unfound hives too: never read them as "known". -> `strategy-hunting-reach.md` "Finding the hive"
- **How do I list the hive systems and worlds a faction knows?** No API: filter `ThreatAttackPlanner.knownSystems()` (found, sector-wide) by `ThreatIntel.known(observer, id)`; the hive worlds seen are the keys of the report's `worlds`. A report holds swarm FP and age only, so sizes, industries and tiers (`SwarmBastion.tier`) are read live from those markets (planet facts stay live by design). Only the player and mobilised factions (`ThreatIntel.observers()`) hold reports. -> `war-council.md` section 10
- **How is a hive found?** The player or a scout entering its system, or any non-Threat colony in the same system (`revealNeighbours`); the find is shared by all factions. A strike gives the struck faction a lead and sorties sweep nearest-first (`ThreatScoutRoute`). `ThreatScouts.reveal` / `leads` -> `strategy-hunting-reach.md` "Finding the hive"
- **Do scouts refresh what is known?** Yes since 2026-10-01: a scout entering a system writes a SCOUT report (`ThreatScouts.onEnter` -> `ThreatIntel.see`), and each faction's planner sends a recon party (`ThreatScouts.recon`, one stop from its nearest base) to a found system whose report is older than `intelHalfLifeDays`, or missing. Before it, no: 20-FP `PATROL_SMALL` fleets sail on leads (a strike's origin, 6 ly) and sweeps every 60 days within `scoutRangeLY` 10; `planRoute` skips discovered systems, and `reveal` appends an id to a bare list - no picture, figure or age is stored. `ThreatScouts.launch`
- **Do forward bases give radar?** Yes since 2026-10-01: forward bases, military worlds and the player's outposts see every found Threat system within `radarRangeLY` (10) once a day, to two significant figures (`ThreatIntel.radarSites`, `advanceDay`). Before it, the only detection was strike warning (`ThreatFrontlines.detectedAt`), which sees strikes in flight, not garrisons. Vanilla sensor arrays add +700 (+400 makeshift) sensor range in-system but reveal no intel; in-system reveal is possible via `setExtendedDetectedAtRange`, cross-system is an engine limit, and a 4 ly picket does not fit 10-36 ly hive geometry. -> `frontlines.md` "3. What is built"
- **How much does a faction trust a report, and who shares it?** Trust = 0.5^((age + travel days) / `intelHalfLifeDays` 30); a plan's chance is 1 - the product of (1 - trust) over its unresolved siege prongs (`ThreatIntel.trust`, `ThreatAttackPlanner.chance`). Coalition partners share reports as they are made, and the player gets those of factions at Cooperative (`ThreatIntel.sharersOf`); `report` returns the newest. -> `attack-planner.md` sections 1 and 11
- **How is a Threat strike detected?** A live fleet visible to the player, in a system with a non-Threat market, or in hyperspace within `strikeDetectLY` 4 of a military world or link (routes at their interpolated position). Detection mobilises every faction with a world in the sweep and gives each one's scouts a lead. `IncursionManager.detectStrikes` -> `frontlines.md` "3. What is built"

## Reach, convoys, hunting, links

- **What is a base's reach?** No radius: reach is the bill. An NPC base reaches as far as its reserve plus pooled `siegeDonors` pay the smallest flotilla (2 fleets of difficulty 5, 250 FP) in passage fuel (100 per ly) and supplies; an empty depot reaches nothing. Player bases use `ThreatColonyManager.fuelRangeLY`. `ThreatReach.baseRangeLY` via `IncursionManager.expeditionRangeLY` -> `strategy-hunting-reach.md` "The factions' reach and stance"
- **How far does one market's stock reach another?** `ThreatConvoys.stockReachLY` = donor fuel above its floor / `haulFuel(1)` (12 fuel per ly). `convoyRangeLY` and `logisticsRangeLY` are gone; player markets reach any range. `IncursionManager.marketsReaching` -> `strategy-convoys.md` "Convoys"
- **What gates a convoy?** Only voyage fuel (`ThreatConvoys.paysVoyage`: `haulFuel(ly)` from the donor's stock above its floor or the fuel it ships). Supplies size the escort and never gate it; no supplies means unescorted. A refusal logs `Convoy held:`. -> `strategy-convoys.md` "Convoys"
- **What does a pool pay without a convoy?** `haulPerUnit`: 0.006 fuel per marine per ly, 0.002 per other unit; fuel pays its own passage. None within a system or from player markets. Used by siege and hunt donors, link upkeep, founding, ordnance. `ThreatConvoys.haulPerUnit` / `netOfHaul` -> `strategy-convoys.md` "Convoys"
- **How is a staging base's stock target set?** `siegeWants` x `stagingTargetMult` 1.5 while `siegeFundable` across the `stockNetwork`, else the smallest hunt's wants; hysteresis 0.8 / 1.0; sieges funded cheapest first. `ThreatConvoys.siegeStock` / `stagingTargets` -> `strategy-convoys.md` "Convoys"
- **What triggers a hunting force?** Each slow tick, per running `ThreatSwarmBountyIntel`, each mobilised NPC tries its bases best-paid first (`huntBases`). Not if it already hunts there, the base sent within `softenIntervalDays` 30, the base has a siegeable hive (siege first), or a hostile faction works the system. Since 2026-10-01, not if the faction has no report of the system, which also stages nothing for it (`ThreatIntel`). `ThreatSoftening.tick` -> `strategy-hunting-reach.md` "Hunting forces"
- **How is a hunt sized and run?** Defense Swarm FP over the siege's worlds x 1.5, at least the garrison now x 1.5, both from the faction's report at face value since 2026-10-01, with no ceiling; fleets of `softenFleetFP` 1500. Musters 3000 units off the anchor, goes in when all arrive or after 15 days, merges to at most 90 ships, retreats below 0.4 strength or after 60 days. `ThreatSoftening.send` / `advanceForce` -> `strategy-hunting-reach.md` "Hunting forces"
- **What pays for a hunt?** Fuel FP/25 x ly x `expeditionFuelPerPointLY`; supplies FP/25 x 30. From stock above the floor and the staging bank, plus donors net of haul; if the swarm blocks the base's own siege, its staging bank too. `ThreatSoftening.donorSpendable` / `huntSpendable` -> `strategy-hunting-reach.md` "Hunting forces"
- **Why does a siege sail right after a hunt?** A hunt that clears a world, or any battle sinking Threat ships in a bountied system, calls `IncursionManager.huntThinned`. Since 2026-10-01 that is news for every faction's planner, so each plans on the next poll instead of waiting out its week; before, it brought the monthly siege pass forward. -> `strategy-hunting-reach.md` "Hunting forces"
- **Why do gathering hunts draw the Threat in?** Posture pressure counts NPC staged stock and sortie capacity, so the Threat reinforces a system while the hunt is still mustering (Epsilon Shero I 621 -> 10.9k FP). Emergent, not a bug. `ThreatPosture.siegeCapacityFP` -> `hive-garrison-and-upkeep.md` "Posture"
- **Does an NPC faction have money?** No. It banks a share of commodity flow each month and pays nothing for it; credit figures in siege logs only weigh razing against invading. `ThreatReserves.accrualPer30` -> `economy-coherence.md` "3. The coherence rules"
- **What caps a faction's fuel banking?** max(what it produces, the sector's best exporter x `reserveBankImportsMult`), so Sindria (~16-18k a month) caps every faction; a forward base adds only with real production. No sector pool exists: cutting Sindria does not starve anyone yet. `ThreatReserves.productionShare`
- **What do NPC fleets pay while out?** Vanilla maintenance per day out (`ThreatFrontlines.maintenancePerMonth`), every 5 days, home base first then `payFromOthers`; a month owed sends the fleet home (`ThreatUpkeep.starve`). Exempt: player, Threat, link garrisons. Repairs and CR are never charged (repairs once billed garrisons 12,901 supplies a month). Vanilla itself charges AI fleets nothing; patrols pay through market demand. `ThreatUpkeep.poll`
- **Why did links once stall toward far hives?** `ThreatFrontlines.planFor` stopped at the first site it could not garrison; it now skips an `unguardable` site and goes down the list (3 -> 24 links per faction). -> `frontlines.md` "3. What is built"
- **What do links build?** From size 3 a Patrol HQ (Military Base at 4) and a battlestation (fortress at 4); in a free slot Fuel Production while `fuelShort` (fuel vs supplies against their staging wants - fuel alone built 24 plants on a 735k-fuel Hegemony), and `swapFuelForHeavyIndustry` one way. They grow by `ThreatFrontlines.feedSize` on the size-upkeep curve; sizes 1-2 are free, so a link never dies of upkeep. -> `frontlines.md`
- **How are NPC Support/Defend sorties sized?** `ThreatFleetOrders.dispatchOrbit` builds `buildSortie` to `siegeOrbitNeeded`, paid in full or stays home (`sortie_unpaid` / `sortie_short`); contested fronts get one from `ThreatConvoys.supportFor`. NPC navies are autonomous; only the player issues board orders. -> `strategy-orbit-outposts.md` "Support, and the refuse rule"
- **How is relief sized?** Threat points over the world x 1.5, built at the nearest base whose depot can provision whole fleets of `reliefFleetFP` 300, no fleet cap; marine convoys carry what beats the army by `siegeBeachheadMargin`. A faction owing relief starts no new siege or planner raid (`reliefOwed` gates only new work: plan, `sailPending`, `cheapestFirst`; nothing already sailing, no hunt or coalition answer, no direct launch). `ThreatFleetOrders.planRelief`, `ThreatGroundFronts.reliefNeed` -> `ground-war-orbit-control.md` "Relief"
- **How are forward bases (links) founded?** `ThreatFrontlines.plan` every 15 days per mobilised NPC: nearest found hive with no base or link within `frontlineReachLY` 10, anchored on the nearest connected colony or link, on an uncolonised planet within `frontlineLinkLY` 12. Must pay `ThreatOutposts.linkCost` and spare a garrison; CONSOLIDATE founds none. -> `frontlines.md` "3. What is built"
- **What does a forward base cost?** `ThreatOutposts.linkCost` = `npcCost` (1,500 supplies + 800 fuel) + `ThreatBuildCost.linkKit` (Population, Spaceport, Waystation, station at vanilla supplies, ~4,000), stood up at once and paid pooled (`ThreatFrontlines.canFund` / `drawFounding`). Later structures at vanilla supplies and build time: Patrol HQ 3,000/60 d, Military Base 4,500/120, Battlestation 5,000/120, Heavy Industry 5,000/120, Fuel Production 4,500/120.
- **What is still free in the human war economy?** Reserves are uncapped with no decay; mobilisation seeds `reserveInitialMonths` 6 free plus a free Waystation; militia marines trickle free (`reserveBaselinePerSize` 5); structures cost nothing to keep ("not now"); stand-down never happens by default (`warModeStandDownDays` 0). `ThreatReserves.accrualPer30`
- **Is a link a base, and what guards it?** Yes (`IncursionManager.isBase` takes any link with a Waystation; `ThreatFrontlines.isOutpost` means a link). A front link keeps the strongest strike in reach x 1.25, at least 200 FP; a rear link only gets the call when a strike is detected. -> `frontlines.md` "3. What is built"

## Ground fronts and landings

- **When does a besieger land?** `readyToLand`: orbit done (`orbitSpent`) or troops x `frontLandingMult` 0.75 >= 0.17 x defence. An NPC first landing also needs `beachheadSurvives` (troops x 0.75 x 2^(1/e) >= counter-attack x 1.25). The player's landing is their call. `ThreatGroundFronts.readyToLand` -> `ground-war-sieges.md` "Sieges from orbit"
- **What are the front states?** HOLDING >= `frontHoldFraction` 0.17 x current defence, GRINDING >= 0.10 (hysteresis 0.1). Effective = troops x entrenchment: 0.75 on landing -> 1.0 over 30 days holding, frozen while pushing, halved on a layer taken or a counter-attack. `ThreatGroundFronts.effectiveStrength` / `entrenchMult` -> `ground-war-fronts.md` "The front"
- **How do fronts wear defences?** HOLDING wears Core/Nexus/port/defence structures and the shield; GRINDING only defences; FOOTHOLD nothing. Rate `frontWearRate` 12 x E/(E+D) days a day, not cut by condition. `ThreatGroundFronts.tickFront` -> `ground-war-fronts.md` "The front"
- **How is a colony's defence figure built?** Garrison (vanilla base x stability x districts held) + armed stockpile marines (`ThreatReserves.armedMarines`, ramp over 20 days, Storage never counts) x fortification multipliers scaled by condition. `ThreatGroundFronts.colonyGarrison` -> `ground-war-defenders.md` "Sieges against human factions"
- **How often does a colony counter-attack?** `frontCounterAttackDays` 40 / max(0.25, stability/10), /1.5 with a military command, / force-ratio tempo (clamped 3x). Marines count x0.25; engaged defenders lose 20% per 30 days plus 15% per counter-attack. `ThreatGroundFronts.counterAttackTempo` / `spendDefendingMarines` -> `ground-war-defenders.md` "Marines defend, and they die"
- **How often does a hive counter-attack?** 40 / `hiveCounterAttackPace` (fed share / break-even x strata not held / size) / tempo; a hive uses its whole body. -> `ground-war-defenders.md` "Marines defend, and they die"
- **How big is a Threat landing?** Pool `strikeTroopsPerPoint` 20 x swarm difficulty points, fixed at launch, cut by surviving strength each pass. Per world max(300, pool/worlds); under 50 the landing is called off. Passes per world = fleets + 2. `ThreatStrikeFGI`, `IncursionManager.expeditionPasses` -> `ground-war-sieges.md` "Sizing the landing"
- **Do Threat fronts use armaments?** No (`needsArms` false unless `threatFrontNeedsArms`). A pushing Threat front takes 2x casualties (`threatPushLossMult`); reinforcement is the next expedition, weighted 4x toward a losing front. -> `ground-war-orbit-control.md` "The swarm does not fight on armaments"
- **Who holds an orbit?** Orbits are per planet: armed non-trader hostiles within 1500 su hold it while their FP >= 0.5 x the besieger's warships there; garrisons are leashed within 700 su. A held orbit blocks landings and every tactical and saturation slice; abstract sieges skip the check (autoresolve weighs every Threat fleet in the system). `orbitContested` (head-count) is only for convoy refusal. `ThreatGroundFronts.orbitHeld` / `orbitContestedFor` -> `ground-war-fronts.md` "NPC fronts"
- **Can marines raid fortifications?** No; only a landed front reaches the guns. On a hive only the Fabrication Core is raidable (`ThreatFortificationRaids.OrganRaid`). -> `suppression-balance.md` "2. No raids on military structures"
- **What happens when the Threat takes a colony?** `convertConquered`: vanilla teardown, then a size-2 hive paid by the nearest live colony's bank; the landing fleet digs in as its garrison. A Threat front kills a colony only by taking its last district. -> `ground-war-defenders.md` "When the colony falls"

## Bombardment, saturation, off-screen fights

- **Who pays in an off-screen fight?** Both. The attacker loses min(0.75, 0.5 x defence/strength) per world; every hostile fleet vanilla counted loses min(0.75, 0.5 x attacker/defence) of its FP. Knob `abstractDefendersFight`. `ThreatAbstractBattle.fought` -> `ground-war-sieges.md` "Off-screen fights cost both sides"
- **How many ways into an off-screen `autoresolve`?** Four: `resolveOnArrival`, `notifySegmentFinished` at the payload's end, vanilla `FGRaidAction.directFleets` once the player is out of spawn range, and vanilla's own damage loop. Guard each against double charging. -> `ground-war-code-paths.md` 1
- **Does the abstract battery toll reach route damage?** No: it lives in `abstractLeft` and the cargo scaling; the spawn count, `refundOnReturn`, `settleLedger`, upkeep and the next world's start read route damage only. -> `ground-war-code-paths.md` 4
- **How often does a watched siege slice?** Per fleet about every 3.3-4.5 d (vanilla `checkRaid`: tracker, 0.5 d hold, 3 d `RECENTLY_PERFORMED_RAID`); `siegeSliceDays` integrates the gap, clamped to [0.5, 10] d, first slice 3. `breaksOff` and the leash run every frame. -> `ground-war-code-paths.md` 3
- **Can the orbit contest weigh an abstract besieger?** Not as is: `orbitHeld` reads the friendly side from real fleets only, so an abstract flotilla counts 0 and any defender holds. Supply its abstract FP. -> `ground-war-code-paths.md` 7
- **How fast does orbit wear fortifications?** Per day per world: rate x F/(F+D) x condition; rate 12 colony, 30 hive. Condition 1->0 over 180 days (colony) / `defenseWearDays` 300 (hive), no floor. `ThreatGroundFronts.siegeSlice` -> `ground-war-sieges.md` "Sieges from orbit"
- **When does a commander stop bombarding?** `orbitSpent`: a day gains less than the 1 d/day repair, takes under `bombardFPWorth` 30 defence per FP lost, or would drop the fleet under 0.33. Worth is waived while NPC troops aboard cannot hold. The swarm values a hull at 44 (`hullWorth`). -> `ground-war-sieges.md` "Bombardment v2"
- **What do the guns cost bombarders?** Per day 0.0008 x the defence Ground Defenses/Heavy Batteries add, smallest ship first, flagship spared; one faction's fleets over one world answer once (`orbitPoints`). Fuel 0.04/FP/day tactical. `ThreatGroundFronts.returnFirePerDay` -> `ground-war-sieges.md` "Bombardment v2"
- **Does saturation raze a hive?** Never. Over a hive it is per-day wear (30 x F/(F+D) x condition x shield), unrest 10, growth paused, 2.86 fuel/FP/day. A hive dies only to a ground victory (`ThreatColonyManager.eradicate`). `ThreatRazing.saturationSlice` -> `ground-war-sieges.md` "Saturation presses a hive"
- **How is razing a human colony priced?** Fuel per level at size s = 10,000 x 10^(s/2) / 144.8 (sizes 1-8: 218 / 909 / 3,093 / 10,000 / 31,841 / 100,910 / 319,320 / 1,010,000), poured at 2.86 x FP a day, cut by shield throughput; a level paid is a size off, story-critical stops at 3.
- **Does the swarm raze human colonies?** Not by default (`strikeSaturationEnabled` false): same engine and 0.0008 gun constant, but it lands a real ground war, pays bombardment fuel from the hive stock, and has only the launch gate at ratio 1.0 (no break-off on arrival, no 1.5x margin). `ThreatStrikeFGI.saturationPass` `ThreatRazing.levelFuel` -> `suppression-balance.md` "4. Saturation bombardment"
- **Bombing squadron vs flotilla?** The flotilla holds orbit; a squadron (least that reaches the stop, stepped x1.5) bombs, chosen by cheapest fuel + trip supplies per day down. `ThreatGroundFronts.squadronPlan` -> `ground-war-sieges.md` "Saturation presses a hive"
- **When does an NPC raze instead of land?** A colony where razing fuel costs less than the landing's marines and arms at base prices; a hive only where its landing is beyond the marines held and it still produces, never with a front on it. `IncursionManager.razeWorlds`, `npcRazeEnabled` -> `ground-war-sieges.md` "Saturation presses a hive"
- **When does Defend vs Support bombard?** Support whenever the orbit is clear; Defend only while its own front cannot hold. Only the Threat fabricates troops from hulls (`defendFabricates`; off with `fabricateDefendEnabled`, which keeps the beachhead's break-up). A navy's Defend goes home when the front is gone, or it is worn below 0.33 and outweighed (`navyHoldsOver`). -> `ground-war-orbit-control.md` "Fabricating troops from the fleet"
- **Who pays ordnance?** The fleet's own fuel, then its supply line's spendable reserve (`payOrdnance`); with none it holds orbit idle. The swarm pays from the hive's fuel stock. -> `suppression-balance.md` "Built 2026-09-28"
- **How does the planetary shield work?** Absorb = 0.75 x shield condition; all other disruption x (1 - absorb); the shield wears from orbit and fronts. `ThreatShield.throughput` -> `ground-war-defenders.md` "The planetary shield"
- **Player bombardment limits?** One a day (`bombardCooldownDays`, shared tactical/saturation); unrest raised to 10 x (1 - condition) tactical, 10 saturation, never stacked. `ThreatincMarketCMD.bombardTactical` -> `suppression-balance.md` "1. Tactical bombardment"

## Hive economy

- **How is a Threat fleet paid for?** From a per-colony FP bank filled by forge SHIPS output x `fabFPPerShipUnit` (100 FP per unit per 30 d), split across nexuses by hulls drawn. No bank, no build. `ThreatColonyManager.fabricationRatePerDay` / `forgeOutput` / `nexusDraw` -> `hive-economy.md` "Fabrication bank"
- **Why is a hive earning 0 FP?** Its Fabrication Core or Swarm Nexus is down or disrupted, no forge reaches it, or the forge is disrupted. More nexuses split the same hulls; only forge output raises the total. `ThreatColonyManager.nexusDraw` -> `hive-economy.md` "Fabrication bank"
- **What stops a rich hive hoarding fleets?** Garrisons cost 0.04 of their FP per 30 d (`garrisonUpkeepPerMonth`), so they settle at income / rate; with the bank negative, `recycleForUpkeep` scraps the weakest for 0.8 of their FP. `ThreatColonyManager.upkeepPerDay` -> `hive-economy.md` "Upkeep"
- **Is there a cap on force size?** No. Bounds are banks, stocks and yards plus engine limits (`maxShipsInAIFleet`, fleet difficulty 0-10, hive `colonyMaxSize` 8). `SIEGE_FLEETS_SANITY` is a loop guard only. -> `design-theory.md` "2a"
- **What do hives stockpile?** Two hive-wide stocks, fuel and supplies, gaining every plant's and forge's whole output x econ unit (fuel 1,500, supplies 750) x `hiveSurplusMult` (1.0; `reserveSurplusMult` until 2026-10-02) a month; no Spaceport demand deducted, nothing imported. `ThreatFuel.perMonth` / `accrue` -> `hive-garrison-and-upkeep.md` "Structures cost supplies"
- **What draws on the hive stocks?** All through `ThreatFuel.pay`: passage, founding cargo, structures, away-fleet supplies, size upkeep, bombardment fuel. Unpaid demand is booked (`heldBuild`, `unmet`) and drives the planner. -> `hive-reach-and-stock.md` "Idle stock"
- **Where does surplus go?** `convertSurplus` turns a surplus fuel plant into a forge, then a missing chain link, then a Swarm Bastion; idle FP buys a Bastion then Command (`militaryIdle`); `retireMilitary` swaps back to production. Surplus: P >= D and S >= D x T. `ThreatFuel.surplus` -> `hive-reach-and-stock.md` "Idle stock"
- **What do Swarm Bastion and Swarm Command give?** The hive's Military Base and High Command (two rows on `SwarmBastion`; needs a Swarm Nexus). `garrisonReserve` = base x (1 + tier): x2 Bastion, x3 Command, staying home and paying upkeep; ground defence x1.2 / x1.3, worn by disruption. `SwarmBastion.tier` -> `hive-reach-and-stock.md` "Idle stock"
- **What do they cost and how long?** Vanilla supply cost at 1.2 supplies per FP: Bastion 3,750 FP, Command 1,250 FP; vanilla's 120-day build. Command is an in-place `startUpgrading`, no slot. `ThreatBuildCost.fleetPoints` / `buildDays` -> `hive-reach-and-stock.md` "Idle stock"
- **When is one built?** `maintainMilitaryTier` / `militaryIdle`: garrison at its posture want, income carries the extra upkeep, pooled banks (`poolableFP`) hold price + `militaryExtraFP` + upkeep over the build, and a free or conversion-freed slot - not while supplies or fuel are wanted and the world lacks that producer. -> `hive-reach-and-stock.md` "Idle stock"
- **When is one torn down?** `retireMilitary`: only if the hive needs a producer (a missing chain link, or a stock `mayAnswer` says runs dry) and no world has a free slot. Bastion before Command, bigger world first; the producer then takes its build time. Skips worlds under a front, saturation or building. No refund. -> `hive-reach-and-stock.md` "Idle stock"
- **How does the planner add or retire plants and forges?** `runsDry`: S < (D - P - C) x T, T = 4 months; one answer per stock per 30 d (`mayAnswer`); spares only if `wantsSpare`. Plants retire only if `surplusProducer` and never the last, biggest, or one with a relic or AI core. Forges never retire. -> `hive-reach-and-stock.md` "Idle stock"
- **Why does a second equal-size producer add nothing?** Availability is a broadcast: each market uses its best single source. Deficits come only from size mismatch or starved inputs. `ThreatColonyManager.planHiveEconomy` -> `hive-economy.md` "Availability is a broadcast"
- **Why is hull supply always short?** Nexus demands ships at size, a forge supplies at size-2; the gap scales the garrison by vanilla's ship-deficit multiplier. Home relics close it. `SwarmNexus.apply`, `maintainHomeRelics` -> `hive-economy.md` "Vanilla industry numbers"
- **How do hives grow and shrink?** Size 3+ costs `sizeUpkeepAt3` 100 x 2.5^(size-3) supplies a month; 1-2 free. Paid in full grows at 60 d x size; below the 0.5 break-even a size is lost per 90 d. Sustenance capped at the largest stance share (`sustainShare`, 0.7); growth gets the stance share (expand 0.5 / press 0.7 / consolidate 0.5; consolidate was 0.9 until round 20). Never desized by razing. `ThreatColonyUpkeep.perMonth` / `growthRate` -> `hive-garrison-and-upkeep.md` "Size upkeep"
- **What does a hive structure cost?** Vanilla credits / 100 x `structureSuppliesMult` in supplies (`buyStructure`); unaffordable builds wait (`buildWaiting`). `foundingFPPerStructure` applies only with `structuresCostSupplies` off. `ThreatBuildCost.supplies` -> `hive-garrison-and-upkeep.md` "Structures cost supplies"
- **What does a strike, raid or scout cost?** No radius (`billedReach`). Passage FP/25 x ly x 10 fuel (0.4 per FP per ly round trip) plus about 0.78 supplies per FP a month away; `canSustain` must hold, so cutting supplies stops launches. `ThreatFuel.passage`, `ThreatReach.canSustain` -> `hive-reach-and-stock.md` "Reach is the bill"
- **What does a Seeding Swarm carry?** 1,500 supplies + 800 fuel (`ThreatOutposts.npcCost`) plus `foundingKit` (about 3,500 supplies) and passage; the new hive starts with an empty bank. Waits while `canFound` fails. -> `hive-garrison-and-upkeep.md` "Size upkeep"
- **When does the swarm open a war with a faction?** Before phase 3 only one already at war, with a grudge (`ThreatAlarm.grudge`), or that never mobilises. Phase 3 needs a size-6+ forge world and a core world the stock can fuel a swarm to. `IncursionManager.warOpen` -> `hive-reach-and-stock.md` "Reach is the bill"
- **How big a garrison does posture want?** `wantFP` = max(base, share of need); need = pressure / 1.5 x `postureMargin` 1.25; base = `garrisonReserve` swarms at the cheapest rows (`minimumFP`) + `LAUNCH_STOCK` 2 rows at a forge colony of size >= min(`spreadMinSize`, `strikeMinSize`); PRESS adds `ThreatStance.extraWantFP`, and triage zeroes a written-off system's need. Surplus goes to pressure transfers, then waves/strikes, then recycling after 150 d. `ThreatPosture.wantFP`, `redistributeByPressure` -> `hive-garrison-and-upkeep.md` "Posture"
- **Does the swarm take a feint?** Narrowly. An attack counts at its target from dispatch; within one posture pass (5-5.6 days) the target's want rises to about 0.83x its pressure, and the deficit moves one whole fleet per donor, same system first, then the nearest. So a feint pulls only if it outweighs what the system holds (by about 1.2x) or sinks swarms. Donors refill from their banks in 1-5 days; a colony that received rests 30 days before it can donate. Staging against a system raises its pressure before anything sails. No reply swarms: the only retaliation is a strike after a hive is eradicated (`IncursionManager.retaliate`). -> `hive-garrison-and-upkeep.md` "How fast it answers", `war-council.md` section 9
- **What can a raid wear down, and what needs saturation?** A raid (`dispatchRaid`, `siegeSlice`) wears the war-strata only: defence structures, the Swarm Nexus and the Bastion or Command (`Theatre.fortifications`). With the Nexus down a colony cannot rebuild garrisons. Forges, ports and fuel plants fall only to saturation (`saturationSlice`), which runs inside a siege expedition (`razePass`, the squadron) or the player's Bombard. -> `war-council.md` section 10
- **What is the Threat's stance?** One sector-wide PRESS / EXPAND / CONSOLIDATE per posture pass; sets `claimCap` via `expansionShare` (1 / 0.5 / 0), PRESS staging want, growth share; dwells 30 d. `ThreatStance.stance` -> `hive-garrison-and-upkeep.md` "Stance"
- **What does disrupting or blockading a hive do?** A disrupted port caps shipping at 3 units and halves imports; a blockade cuts imports half or all (`ThreatBlockade.hiveCut`); a raided forge stops stock and growth. `applyPortDisruption`, `ThreatPurgeFGI.raidValue` -> `hive-reach-and-stock.md` "Levers, verified"
- **Why do importers read an old figure after a build?** Vanilla recomputes supply monthly; structural changes call `markEconomyDirty` and `flushEconomy` runs `tripleStep` once a tick. -> `hive-economy.md` "The monthly lag"
- **How do NPC reserves fill?** Per 30 d: `getSizeMult(surplus over peacetime demand)` x econ unit x `reserveSurplusMult`; troops at 0.5. Mobilised, the reserve covers a colony's peacetime deficit first, up to 0.5 per issue. `ThreatReserves.accrualPer30` / `coverShortage` -> `economy-coherence.md` "3. The coherence rules"

Rep = value / 10,000, cap +10. Plus missions, in-person hand-over and swarm bounties. `ThreatAid.canAid` / `onDelivered` -> `player-aid.md` "2. Player aid"

## Threat fleets

- **How does the swarm decide to strike?** Once a month (`IncursionManager.tick` -> `tryStrikes`, phase >= 2), each hive system in shuffled order gets one pass: `pickStrikeStaging` (biggest colony, size >= 4, ships, >= 2 spare swarms, fuel, a working Nexus) -> `pickStrikeTarget` (weight size squared x grudge / strikeDays x stance; a dry Threat front wins outright) -> `launchStrike`. No coordination, no cap, no plan object. -> `swarm-strategy.md` section 1
- **How big is a swarm strike?** Everything above the reserve (`peekMuster`), not sized on the target; trimmed by fuel (`ThreatFuel.canPay`), supplies (`ThreatReach.canSustain`) and the bank. h53d: 23 strikes of 297-6,694 FP (mean ~2.2k), 13 of them within 4 ly. -> `swarm-strategy.md` section 1
- **Why does the swarm never take a human world (h53d)?** It scatters: each hive system spends fuel on its own monthly strike, the hive's fuel stock sits at 3-3,000 against 50-77k a month spent (up to 31 sends held a month), so no front gets follow-up waves. 11 landings, 5 beachheads overrun, 7 strata, 0 worlds in 14 months. The offence-led fix is the Hive Mind design. -> `swarm-strategy.md` sections 2-3
- **Does CONSOLIDATE stop the swarm spreading?** Only new outward claims. `tryExpandInSystem` waves, matured claims (`advanceStages`) and conversions go on; the real cap is one `pickSpreadTarget` claim per tick plus `seedToColonyDays` 120 (h53d: 5 waves launched under CONSOLIDATE). -> `swarm-strategy.md` section 1
- **Are the swarm's harassment and saturation passes live?** No: both need `strikeSaturationEnabled` (false), so the frontier harassment strike is dormant. A short landing share breaks hulls into troops (`beachheadLanding`, `fabricateTroopsPerFP`: 151 FP became 1,510 troops at Salamanca). -> `swarm-strategy.md` section 1
- **How are Threat fleets composed?** `ThreatFleetComposer` re-spends vanilla's escort FP per archetype (host, vanguard, battery, tide, hunter, scout) for jobs strike, garrison, seeding, scout. `ThreatFleetComposer.pickArchetype` -> `fleet-archetypes.md` "How a fleet is composed"

## Traps that cost hours

- **Why is 0 a bad "never" for a day?** The calendar's day numbers are negative: `ThreatPosture.today()` (`getElapsedDaysSince(1L)`) is about -642,000 in cycle 206, so a 0 or -1000 sentinel lies in the future. h50a's raids never sailed: `ThreatAttackPlanner.siegeArrival` returned 0 for "no siege", and every raid waited for day 0. Use `-Float.MAX_VALUE`.
- **Assert from the code or the log, never memory.** "Links build no industry", "552k fuel at Chicomoztoc" (a 16-market pool), "vanilla NPCs pay nothing for supplies" and a guessed "circular hunt exclusion" were all wrong and cost the user's trust.
- **A shortage rule that answers any unmet demand overbuilds** (20 fuel plants in one tick, later 59 never retired). Pace answers, build retirement with them, and compare a stock against the other commodity, not against wants that stay "short".
- **One lever per run, and check the opposite resource**: links building fuel first fixed fuel and starved supplies.
- **A frozen number is a clock bug, not AI**: a pressure stuck at 6,442 was `getElapsedDaysSince(0L)`; a static 30-day timer never fired. Log raw numbers early.
- **Review before the long test**: after a big change a read-only review found statics surviving load, 100,000-fleet siege lists, quadratic re-sorts and a doubled refund.
- **Builder agents die on API errors** (ENOTFOUND, self-signed cert): resume with SendMessage. Give builders disjoint files, and give each task its own scratch subfolder (shared names like `ex.pl` got overwritten).
- **Pool membership reads `ThreatReserves.available` only.** Reading spendable, spare, `escortStock`, `stagingBank` or `expeditionRangeLY` inside logistic reach recurses without bound.
- **A new gate can starve a flow silently**: gating convoys on escort supplies cut them 82 -> 17. Diff a counts table against a baseline log, and make gate and reach read the same stock measure (mixing them took stand-downs 17 -> 47 in h45a).
- **Planner and executor share one pricing function**: the squadron verdict priced with `Float.MAX_VALUE` fuel, then gated all-or-nothing, hid smaller squadrons and left the runtime path untested.
- **Ask before skipping a balanced engine**: the one-shot 300-day "wreck" read to the user as a regression.
- **Do not assume a mechanic the user remembers exists**, and do not deny one either: check, then answer with numbers before building.
- **Grep the raw `starsector.log` for exceptions**: `extract.ps1` drops Exception/ERROR lines.
- **Subagent hand-backs are model output**, never the user's decisions.
